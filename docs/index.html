Download for macOS

Features

Spin the wheel

Roll your mouse wheel to flip through every image in a folder, the way image viewers used to work. Hold ⌘ and scroll to zoom. Pinch on the trackpad if you've moved on.

Open an image — that's the library

No imports. No catalog. No "would you like to sync." Open any image and you're already browsing its folder. Sort by name, date, or size, and reverse it whenever you want.

Every format that matters

JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL, SVG, PSD — and camera RAW from Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fuji, Panasonic, Olympus, Pentax, Sigma, Hasselblad, Leica and more. One viewer for everything in the folder.

Quietly native

Built in AppKit for modern macOS. Light, quick, and yours — no account, no telemetry, no cloud. Just an app that opens images.

Northlight is free and we don't track anything. Files stay on your Mac. Built with passion for people who miss when looking at a folder of photos was a single click away.
